# Cream & olive design direction
SUBJECT: Islam Medical Complex patients finding a specialist and contacting reception.
GROUND: warm limestone reception spaces, printed consultant directories, bronze wayfinding, quiet consultation rooms.
PALETTE: ivory #F6F1E7 ground; paper #FFFCF5 surface; oat #E8DECB dividers and dark-surface accents; forest #303C35 ink/deep sections; bronze #806044 accents; warm grey #706A5D secondary ink. Neutral ramps have a yellow-brown bias.
TYPE: Manrope 700 for clear, open specialist names and headings; Manrope 400–600 for schedules and navigation; Newsreader for brief editorial accents, giving warmth without compromising the roster's readability.
SPACE: 8/12/20px within controls; 80/112px section rhythm. Keep the specialist directory compact enough to compare names.
SHAPE: 16px principal surfaces, nested 12px images, pill-shaped action controls. Separation primarily by tonal contrast; remove decorative glow blobs.
MOTION: preserve existing scroll and slideshow behavior; add five interaction details—traced accent strokes, directory hover rules, selection rules/photo settling, navigation underlines, and dialog arrivals. Controls 220–420ms; focal photo/accent 650–900ms. Reduced motion uses brief opacity feedback.
SIGNATURE: the consultant directory treated like hospital wayfinding, with readable bold names and a fine rule that follows focus.
CRITIQUE: cream plus serif alone could resemble a hospitality template. Keep the principal voice bold sans-serif and retain the real hospital photography and clinical directory; restrict the serif to existing editorial areas and retain the existing logo mark.
