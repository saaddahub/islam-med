# Islam Medical Complex

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Patients browse care, doctors and reception information. Clinic staff with little technical experience maintain public website content through an admin area.

## Product Purpose

Present the medical complex and let staff update text, photographs and doctor profiles without editing source code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing React, TypeScript and Vite website. The owner selected Appwrite for database, image storage and admin authentication, with saved drafts followed by explicit publication. Appointment actions provide reception contact information; patient records and appointment processing are outside this content-management task.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing website's taste and identity. Make administration understandable for beginners.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing public content in src/data and src/components, clinic photography in public, and the existing website's ivory, ink-blue and editorial serif visual system.

## Product Principles

- Organize editing around recognizable website sections.
- Separate draft changes from public content.
- Require authorized staff access for shared content changes.
- Preserve existing public content until replacement content is published.
